# Shein Tracking Statuses Explained: Customs, In Transit & Out for Delivery

Shein tracking messages can look inconsistent because wording comes from the merchant app, linehaul partners, customs systems, and last-mile couriers. Understanding each stage tells you whether to wait, prepare for delivery, or contact support. Track your number anytime on [**SheinTracking.com**](/).

## Order placed / processing / preparing

Your payment cleared and the warehouse is building the parcel. There may be **no carrier tracking number** yet. This is normal for 1–5 days depending on stock and campaign volume.

## Shipped / in transit / departed origin facility

The package left the seller or SHEIN warehouse network. International scans may appear sparsely. Quiet gaps of a few days between hubs are common.

## Arrived at transit hub / airline departure / arrived overseas

The parcel is moving through air or regional sorting. Timestamps can jump time zones. Keep the same tracking number — it usually stays valid end to end.

## Undergoing customs clearance / import customs

Border agencies are processing the shipment. This is one of the most searched Shein statuses. Most parcels clear without action from you; duties or holds are less common for typical small fashion orders but possible by country. Details: [Shein customs clearance](/shein-customs-clearance).

## Customs released / clearance completed

The parcel is cleared for local delivery and often handed to a postal or courier partner. Watch for a new facility name in your country.

## Arrived at local facility / destination sorting center

Your package is inside the domestic network. Updates should become more frequent from here.

## Out for delivery

A courier has the parcel on a delivery route for that day. Ensure someone can receive it or that safe-place / locker instructions are set.

## Delivered / collected from pickup point

Scan shows completion. If nothing arrived, follow [delivered but not received](/shein-delivered-but-not-received) before disputing.

## Exception / delayed / returned to sender

Something interrupted the plan — weather, address issue, failed attempt, or return. Read the event text carefully and contact the carrier plus SHEIN support with your order ID and tracking number.

## Why the same order shows different words

SHEIN’s app summarizes merchant milestones. Carrier trackers show operational scans. Both can be correct at once. When in doubt, trust the latest detailed event on [Shein Tracking](/) and the last-mile carrier site.
