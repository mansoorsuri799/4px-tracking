# About Us

Welcome to [sheintracking.com](/), your independent destination for real-time **Shein tracking** and international shipment updates. Online fashion shopping should feel exciting — not stressful. Our mission is simple: make tracking Shein orders and partner-carrier parcels easy, accurate, and hassle-free.

## Who We Are

Shein Tracking is an independent multi-carrier tracking site. We are **not** SHEIN, and we are not affiliated with SHEIN or its parent companies. We aggregate publicly available shipment scan data so shoppers can follow one tracking number from warehouse handoff through customs and last-mile delivery.

## What We Offer

- Fast tracking lookup on the homepage
- Multi-carrier support for networks commonly used on Shein parcels
- Clear guides on delivery times, statuses, customs, and missing packages
- Practical FAQs written for real shopper problems

## Why Shoppers Use SheinTracking.com

SHEIN’s app shows order context; carrier networks show operational scans. We bridge both by focusing on the tracking number itself — with plain-language guides when the timeline looks confusing.

## Our Commitment

We aim for honest timelines, original editorial content, and transparent disclaimers. Tracking data depends on carrier scans; quiet gaps happen. When something looks wrong, our guides explain the next step without hype.
