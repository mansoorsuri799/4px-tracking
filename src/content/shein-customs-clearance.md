# Shein Package Undergoing Customs Clearance: How Long It Takes

Search threads light up whenever a Shein timeline flips to **package undergoing customs clearance**. Shoppers worry the parcel is seized or lost. In reality, this status usually means border processing is underway before local delivery. Here is what it means, how long it often takes, and when to act. Watch the live events on [**SheinTracking.com**](/).

## What “undergoing customs clearance” means

Your shipment reached the destination country’s import process. Agencies (or their appointed brokers) review the parcel before releasing it to the domestic network. Fashion and small-goods orders frequently clear without you paying anything extra — but rules vary by country and declared value.

## How long does Shein customs clearance take?

| Scenario | Typical experience |
| --- | --- |
| Routine clearance | Often **about 1–7 days** |
| Busy seasons / sales spikes | Can stretch longer |
| Physical inspection selected | Add extra days to a couple of weeks |
| Incomplete docs / duties due | Held until resolved |

Community reports (including Reddit Shein threads) often cite roughly **5–7 days** as a common wait before the next local scan — not a hard rule. After release you should see wording like customs released, arrived at local facility, or a named postal partner.

## What happens after customs release?

The parcel hands off to a last-mile carrier. Tracking may briefly look quiet again during that handoff, then resume with domestic facility scans. Use the same number on [Shein Tracking](/) and the local courier site.

## Do you need to pay or do anything?

Most of the time: **no**. Only respond to fee or document requests that come through **official** SHEIN or government/carrier channels. Ignore random SMS links claiming your “Shein package is held for customs payment.”

If a legitimate duty notice appears, pay or respond through the verified portal listed by the carrier or customs agency for your country.

## When should you contact support?

Reach out if:

- Customs wording has not changed for an unusually long stretch past your delivery estimate
- The status flips to exception, returned, or seized
- You received an official duty notice you do not understand

Provide order ID, tracking number, and screenshots from your timeline.
