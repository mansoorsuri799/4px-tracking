# How to Track Your Shein Order: Step-by-Step Guide

Waiting on a fashion haul is easier when you know exactly where the parcel is. **Shein tracking** usually starts with a number in My Orders, then continues across international hubs, customs, and a local courier. This guide shows every practical way to track a Shein package in 2026 — including on [**SheinTracking.com**](/).

## Where to find your Shein tracking number

1. Open the SHEIN website or app and sign in.
2. Go to **My Orders** (often under the Me / Account icon).
3. Select the order that shows as **Shipped** or **In transit**.
4. Tap **Track** or **View details** to copy the shipment / tracking number and carrier name when shown.

If the order is still processing, a carrier tracking number may not exist yet. Warehouse packing and handoff often take one to a few days after payment clears.

## Track on SheinTracking.com (fastest multi-carrier view)

1. Copy the tracking number from My Orders.
2. Open [sheintracking.com](/) and paste it into the search box.
3. Optionally tap **Choose Carrier** if you know the network (Cainiao, Yun Express, USPS, and others are common for Shein parcels).
4. Submit to see status, origin/destination hints, and the scan timeline.

Universal trackers help when SHEIN’s in-app view is sparse or when the parcel has already moved to a last-mile partner.

## Track inside the SHEIN app or website

SHEIN’s own order page is the best place for merchant-side milestones (paid, processed, shipped). After shipping, the Track button usually deep-links or displays the same carrier number you can paste here. Use both: SHEIN for order context, [Shein Tracking](/) for denser scan history.

## Track with the last-mile carrier

Once the status mentions a local facility, post office, or named courier, open that carrier’s official site with the same number. Last-mile scans (out for delivery, attempted delivery, locker) often appear there first.

## Tips for accurate Shein order tracking

- Copy the number carefully — no spaces; letters and digits only.
- Expect 3–5 day quiet periods between some international checkpoints.
- Split orders can produce **multiple** tracking numbers; check each package.
- Marketplace / third-party seller orders may ship on different timelines than SHEIN warehouse stock.
