# Shein Says Delivered But I Didn't Receive It? What to Do

Few tracking moments feel worse than **Delivered** with an empty doorstep. SHEIN’s help center covers this exact case, and most outcomes are mundane: wrong porch, neighbor, locker, or building office — not theft. Use this checklist before you panic, then escalate with evidence. Verify the scan details on [**SheinTracking.com**](/).

## What “Delivered” usually means

The last-mile carrier recorded a completion event. That might be:

- Handed to a person at the address
- Left in a safe place / porch / garage
- Dropped at a pickup point, locker, or reception desk
- Marked delivered early by mistake (less common, but it happens)

Read the full event text — many scans include a photo note, locker code hint, or “left with…” detail.

## Immediate checks (same day)

1. Walk the property: side door, back porch, bins, parcel lockers.
2. Ask household members, neighbors, and building staff / concierge.
3. Check spam for pickup notices or carrier emails/SMS.
4. Confirm the **shipping address** on the SHEIN order matches where you expected delivery.
5. Open the carrier’s tracking page with the same number for proof-of-delivery notes.

## Contact the last-mile carrier

Use the courier named in the latest scan. Provide:

- Tracking number
- Delivery address
- Date/time of the delivered event
- Your contact phone

Ask them to open a **trace / missing package** ticket. Do this quickly — many carriers have short investigation windows.

## Contact SHEIN support

If the carrier cannot locate the parcel:

1. Open the order in the SHEIN app → help / contact support.
2. Attach screenshots from [Shein Tracking](/) and the carrier site.
3. Explain the searches you already completed.
4. Request a reshipment or refund per SHEIN’s policy for your region.

SHEIN’s FAQ for “marked as delivered but not received” is the policy path to follow alongside the carrier claim.

## Protect future deliveries

- Add delivery instructions where the carrier allows them.
- Use workplace or locker addresses for high-value hauls.
- Enable carrier email/SMS alerts when offered.
- Track earlier so you know the out-for-delivery day — see [how to track](/how-to-track-your-shein-order).
