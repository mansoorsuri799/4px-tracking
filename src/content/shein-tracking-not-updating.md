# Shein Tracking Not Updating? Causes and What to Do

Reddit and Facebook threads fill up whenever a Shein timeline freezes — “loading complete,” “in transit,” or no new scan for days. Official SHEIN help notes that **tracking updates can take 3–5 days** between checkpoints because scans only happen at facilities. This guide explains why **Shein tracking not updating** is often normal, when to dig deeper, and how to verify the number on [**SheinTracking.com**](/).

## Why Shein tracking pauses

### 1. The number is brand new
After “shipped,” the first meaningful carrier scan can lag while the parcel moves from warehouse to linehaul.

### 2. Long international legs
Air and ocean segments may not produce daily scans. A multi-day silence between origin and destination hubs is routine.

### 3. Customs processing
Statuses like undergoing customs clearance can sit unchanged while paperwork or inspections finish. See [customs clearance](/shein-customs-clearance).

### 4. Carrier handoff
When the parcel switches to USPS or another local courier, the parent tracker sometimes lags until the partner posts a scan.

### 5. Wrong or incomplete number
Extra spaces, missing digits, or confusing an order ID with a tracking number will show stale or empty results.

## How long should you wait?

| Situation | Reasonable wait before escalating |
| --- | --- |
| Just shipped, no scans yet | Often **1–5 days** |
| Quiet gap mid-transit | Often **3–5 days** between hubs |
| Stuck in customs wording | Several days; longer in peak season |
| Past the estimated delivery window by a week+ | Contact SHEIN support with proof |

## Proven fixes when tracking stalls

1. **Re-copy** the tracking number from My Orders → Track.
2. Paste it on [sheintracking.com](/) and, if known, select the carrier.
3. Cross-check the same number on the last-mile courier site once a local facility appears.
4. Confirm you are not looking at a **different package** from a split shipment.
5. Screenshot the timeline and message SHEIN support if the estimate has clearly expired.

## What not to do

- Do not pay unexpected “customs fee” links from random SMS or email — use official SHEIN channels.
- Do not assume the package is lost after two quiet days on an international route.
- Do not open a chargeback on day three; gather tracking evidence first.
